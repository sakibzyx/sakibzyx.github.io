About Me

Hi, I'm Md Sakibul Hasan, a passionate undergraduate student of Civil Engineering at the Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET) and a Diploma graduate in Civil Technology from Sylhet Polytechnic Institute (2023). I am always open to connecting with engineers, researchers, students, and industry professionals to collaborate, share knowledge, and explore opportunities in civil engineering and sustainable infrastructure.
