About Me

Hi, I'm Md Sakibul Hasan, a passionate undergraduate student of Civil Engineering at the Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET) and a Diploma graduate in Civil Engineering from Sylhet Polytechnic Institute (2023). My interests include structural engineering, structural analysis, earthquake engineering, seismic-resistant design, and sustainable infrastructure. I am passionate about developing innovative, resilient, and sustainable engineering solutions while continuously strengthening my technical and analytical skills.

Beyond academics, I serve as the Finance Secretary of the DUET Journalist Association and a Volunteer Committee Member of Bijoy 24 Hall, DUET, where I contribute to financial planning and organizational activities. I have also mentored 700+ students in Physics, Mathematics, and Engineering, reflecting my commitment to leadership, education, and community development.

I am always open to connecting with engineers, researchers, students, and industry professionals to collaborate, share knowledge, and explore opportunities in civil engineering and sustainable infrastructure.
